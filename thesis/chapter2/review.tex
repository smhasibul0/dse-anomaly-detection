\section{Critical Analysis and Research Gaps}
% From the Gaps tab of the literature table. Check each against the full texts.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item GARCH combined with deep learning is used almost only for volatility forecasting, not anomaly detection.
    \item Financial anomaly studies rarely control for volatility clustering, so detectors may flag regime shifts as anomalies.
    \item There is no deep anomaly detection work on the DSE; DSE literature is GARCH-only and in-sample.
    \item Cross-market transfer has been studied for trading and prediction, not for anomaly detection into a frontier market.
    \item Financial anomaly labels are tiny or purely synthetic.
    \item Point-adjust inflation is documented on industrial benchmarks but not measured on financial data.
    \item Standard time-series anomaly benchmarks contain no financial series.
\end{enumerate}
