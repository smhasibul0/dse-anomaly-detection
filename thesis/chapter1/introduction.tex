\section{Scope and Delimitation}
The study uses daily data only, because free intraday DSE data do not exist. On the DSE it
covers the DSEX index from its launch in January 2013 and 10 to 20 liquid stocks chosen by
turnover. Periods under BSEC floor prices are treated as a separate regime. Global markets
serve as the source domain for transfer. Market manipulation cases, order-book data and
news or text data are outside the scope. \todo{Name the global indices and final date ranges.}
